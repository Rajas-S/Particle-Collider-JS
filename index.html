To maximise performance: it is recommended to close other tabs.

Enabling ParticleRect, Disabling Colour and GridShow, and using a smaller particle radius for higher particle numbers (e.g. radius 1 for 100,000 particles) also helps.

if you experience a drop in framerate after prolonged use, try closing the tab for a few minutes.

On lower framerates you may need to hold down on GUI elements to register an update.

Lower SimSpeed value are recommended for more physically accurate collisions when there is non-zero acceleration.
